Breakdown of Ergodicity in Many-Body Systems

January 25–27, 2027 • SISSA, Trieste, Italy

About the Workshop Invited Speakers
Overview

Scope of the Workshop

The workshop focuses on the breakdown of ergodicity in classical and quantum many-body systems. We are interested in understanding when and why many-body systems fail to thermalize, or take very long times to reach thermal equilibrium. Topics include near-integrable dynamics, many-body localization, anomalous transport, and the persistence of memory of the initial state. The workshop aims to bring together researchers working on classical and quantum systems, with the goal of finding common ideas, understanding important differences, and discussing open problems in non-equilibrium many-body physics.

Registration

Interested participants are requested to register for the workshop by submitting the registration form before December 15, 2026.

Participants are also encouraged to contribute to the workshop with a poster presentation. Please submit a short abstract of up to 200 words describing the work you would like to present using the registration form. Poster contributions related to the theme of the workshop are welcome.
